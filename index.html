💊 Supplements & Hydration

Essential Supplements

• Whey protein powder (2-3 scoops daily)
• Creatine monohydrate (5g daily)
• Vitamin B12 (1000mcg weekly)
• Vitamin D (get tested first)
• Omega-3 (algae-based or flaxseeds)

Hydration

• Drink 3-4 liters of water daily
• More water on workout days
• Monitor urine color (should be pale yellow)
• Drink water with each meal

⚠️ Important Notes

Training is Essential

You MUST start resistance training 3-5x per week. Without training, extra calories become fat, not muscle. Focus on compound movements: squats, deadlifts, bench press, rows, overhead press.

Progress Tracking

• Weigh yourself weekly (same time)
• Target: 0.25-0.5kg weight gain per week
• Track protein intake daily
• Adjust calories if needed after 2 weeks

Meal Prep Tips

• Cook dal/rajma/chana in bulk
• Boil eggs in advance
• Keep paneer and yogurt stocked
• Pre-portion nuts and protein powder

Sleep & Recovery

Get 7-9 hours of quality sleep nightly. Poor sleep significantly impairs muscle protein synthesis and recovery.
